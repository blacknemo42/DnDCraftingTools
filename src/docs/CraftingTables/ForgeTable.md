The Forge Table is used for blacksmithing weapons and armor. It requires specialized tools, materials, and a heat source.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Smith's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Smith's tools
- **Ability Check**: Strength (Athletics)

## Crafting Process
1. Gather required metals and materials
2. Heat the forge to proper temperature
3. Shape the metal according to design specifications
4. Make a Strength (Athletics) check against the DC of the item
5. Complete the cooling and finishing process

## Special Rules
- Failed checks may result in wasted materials
- Working with different metals requires different techniques
- Some items require additional components from other crafting disciplines
- Magical weapons and armor require additional enchanting steps

## Common Materials
- Iron and steel
- Precious metals (gold, silver, mithral)
- Monster parts for special properties
- Coal or other fuel sources
- Quenching oils and polishing materials
