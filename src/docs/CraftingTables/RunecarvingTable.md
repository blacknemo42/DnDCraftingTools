The Runecarving Table is used for inscribing magical runes onto objects to imbue them with power. It requires specialized tools and knowledge of ancient runic languages.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Engraver's tools, Calligrapher's supplies
- **Proficiency Required**: Arcana
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Arcana)

## Crafting Process
1. Prepare the surface of the object to be inscribed
2. Design the runic pattern based on desired effect
3. Carefully engrave the runes with precise strokes
4. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check against the DC of the rune
5. Activate the rune with a magical ritual

## Craftable Items

### Minor Runes (DC 12)
- Rune of Durability (50 gp) - Object becomes resistant to wear and tear
- Rune of Identification (50 gp) - Object bears the owner's mark that only they can see
- Rune of Light (75 gp) - Object sheds light as a torch when activated
- Rune of Warning (100 gp) - Object vibrates when danger is near
- Rune of Warmth (75 gp) - Object radiates heat in cold environments
- Rune of Waterproofing (50 gp) - Object repels water and stays dry
- Rune of Language (100 gp) - Inscribed text can be read in any language
- Rune of Returning (100 gp) - Object can be called back to the owner's hand (within 30 feet)

### Standard Runes (DC 15)
- Rune of Sharpness (200 gp) - Weapon deals +1 damage
- Rune of Protection (250 gp) - Armor or shield grants +1 AC
- Rune of Feather Fall (200 gp) - Bearer falls slowly when activated
- Rune of Fire Resistance (300 gp) - Object and bearer resist fire damage
- Rune of Frost Resistance (300 gp) - Object and bearer resist cold damage
- Rune of Climbing (200 gp) - Grants advantage on climbing checks
- Rune of Swimming (200 gp) - Grants swimming speed equal to walking speed
- Rune of Alarm (250 gp) - Creates an audible alarm when triggered
- Rune of Sealing (300 gp) - Creates a magical lock on a container or door

### Greater Runes (DC 18)
- Rune of Elemental Warding (1,000 gp) - Resistance to one elemental damage type
- Rune of Spell Storing (1,500 gp) - Can store one spell of 3rd level or lower
- Rune of Flying (1,200 gp) - Grants flying speed of 30 feet for 10 minutes per day
- Rune of Smiting (1,000 gp) - Weapon deals an extra 1d8 radiant damage
- Rune of Fortification (1,200 gp) - 25% chance to negate critical hits
- Rune of Mind Shielding (1,000 gp) - Protects against mind reading and charm effects
- Rune of Teleportation (1,500 gp) - Can teleport to a predetermined location once per day
- Rune of Seeing (1,200 gp) - Grants darkvision 60 feet
- Rune of Truth (1,000 gp) - Glows when lies are spoken nearby

### Master Runes (DC 20)
- Rune of Giant Strength (5,000 gp) - Sets Strength score to 21
- Rune of Absorption (5,500 gp) - Absorbs spells of 4th level or lower
- Rune of Regeneration (6,000 gp) - Regenerate 5 hit points per round for 1 minute
- Rune of Spell Turning (5,000 gp) - 20% chance to reflect spells back at caster
- Rune of Planar Binding (5,500 gp) - Binds an elemental or outsider to service
- Rune of Life Preservation (6,000 gp) - Automatically stabilizes the bearer when they drop to 0 hit points
- Rune of Phasing (5,000 gp) - Can move through solid objects for 1 minute per day
- Rune of Commanding (5,500 gp) - Advantage on Charisma checks against specific creature types

### Legendary Runes (DC 25)
- Rune of Invulnerability (25,000 gp) - Immunity to nonmagical damage for 1 minute per day
- Rune of Wish Storing (30,000 gp) - Can cast Wish once, then becomes inert
- Rune of Resurrection (25,000 gp) - Can cast Resurrection once per week
- Rune of True Seeing (25,000 gp) - Grants truesight 120 feet for 10 minutes per day
- Rune of Titan Strength (25,000 gp) - Sets Strength score to 29 for 1 minute per day
- Rune of Spell Immunity (30,000 gp) - Complete immunity to one specific spell
- Rune of Immortality (50,000 gp) - Bearer does not age while attuned

## Special Rules
- The crafter must be able to cast at least one spell
- Failed checks may result in unpredictable magical effects
- Different materials hold runes differently (stone is most stable)
- Runes can be combined for more complex effects
- Some runes require special inks made from magical components

## Common Materials
- Special inks and pigments
- Engraving tools of various sizes
- Precious metals for inlaying
- Magical essences for activation
- Reference texts of ancient runes
