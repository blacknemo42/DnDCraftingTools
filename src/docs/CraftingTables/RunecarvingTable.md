# Runecarving Table

The Runecarving Table is used for inscribing magical runes on various items. It requires specialized tools and arcane knowledge.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Jeweler's tools and Calligrapher's supplies
- **Proficiency Required**: Arcana skill
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Arcana)

## Crafting Process

1. Prepare the surface for inscription
2. Create special inks or engraving tools
3. Inscribe the runes with precise arcane patterns
4. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check against the DC of the rune
5. Complete the activation and binding process

## Crafting DCs

| Rune Type       | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|-----------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Minor Rune      | 15          | 3 days        | 100       |
| Standard Rune   | 18          | 7 days        | 500       |
| Greater Rune    | 20          | 14 days       | 2,000     |
| Master Rune     | 25          | 30 days       | 10,000    |
| Legendary Rune  | 30          | 60 days       | 50,000    |

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in a rune with unpredictable effects
- Different materials react differently to runic inscriptions
- Runes can be combined for enhanced or multiple effects
- Some runes require specific environmental conditions to inscribe

## Common Materials

- Special inks made from magical components
- Engraving tools for stone, metal, wood, etc.
- Precious gems and metals as focusing elements
- Binding agents
- Activation catalysts
