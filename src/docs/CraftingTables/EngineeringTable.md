The Engineering Table is used for creating mechanical devices, traps, and siege weapons. It requires specialized tools and technical knowledge.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Tinker's tools, Smith's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Tinker's tools
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Investigation)

## Crafting Process
1. Design the mechanical device or structure
2. Gather required materials and components
3. Assemble the components according to design
4. Make an Intelligence (Investigation) check against the DC of the device
5. Test and refine the mechanism

## Special Rules
- Complex devices may require multiple skill checks during construction
- Failed checks may result in malfunctioning devices
- Some devices require regular maintenance to function properly
- Advanced and masterwork devices may require rare or magical components
- Knowledge of mathematics and physics is essential for complex designs

## Common Materials
- Metal components (gears, springs, levers)
- Wood and stone for structural elements
- Rope and chain for mechanical systems
- Specialized tools for fine adjustments
- Lubricants and preservatives
- Magical components for enhanced functionality
