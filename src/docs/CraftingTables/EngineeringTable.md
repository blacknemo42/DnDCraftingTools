# Engineering Table

The Engineering Table is used for building complex mechanical devices. It requires specialized tools, technical knowledge, and various materials.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Tinker's tools and Smith's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Tinker's tools
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Investigation)

## Crafting Process

1. Design the mechanical device with detailed schematics
2. Gather required materials and components
3. Assemble the device according to the design
4. Make an Intelligence (Investigation) check against the DC of the device
5. Test and refine the mechanism

## Crafting DCs

| Device Type     | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|-----------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Simple Device   | 12          | 3 days        | 50        |
| Complex Device  | 15          | 7 days        | 200       |
| Advanced Device | 18          | 14 days       | 1,000     |
| Masterwork      | 22          | 30 days       | 5,000     |
| Revolutionary   | 25          | 60 days       | 20,000    |

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in a malfunctioning device
- Different materials provide different properties
- Some devices require additional components from other crafting disciplines
- Magical devices require additional enchanting steps

## Common Materials

- Various metals (iron, steel, brass, etc.)
- Gears, springs, and other mechanical components
- Specialized tools for precise work
- Lubricants and binding agents
- Power sources (magical or mundane)

*Reference: Crafting 1.0.6 - Free Version*
