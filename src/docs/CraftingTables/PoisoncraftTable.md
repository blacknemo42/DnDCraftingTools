The Poisoncraft Table is used for creating poisons and venoms. It requires specialized tools, ingredients, and careful handling.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Poisoner's kit
- **Proficiency Required**: Poisoner's kit
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Nature)

## Crafting Process
1. Gather required toxic ingredients
2. Prepare the workspace with proper ventilation and safety measures
3. Follow the recipe precisely
4. Make an Intelligence (Nature) check against the DC of the poison
5. Complete the brewing process

## Crafting DCs
| Poison Type | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|

## Craftable Items

### Basic Poisons (DC 10)
- Crawler Mucus (200 gp) - The target's speed is halved for 1 minute
- Hunting Poison (40 gp) - Deals 1d4 poison damage when applied to a weapon
- Miner's Lure (25 gp) - Attracts vermin and small creatures
- Rat Poison (20 gp) - Kills small vermin, sickens larger creatures
- Sleep Toxin (50 gp) - Target must make a DC 10 Constitution save or become drowsy

### Injury Poisons (DC 15)
- Basic Poison (100 gp) - Target must make a DC 10 Constitution save or take 1d4 poison damage
- Serpent Venom (200 gp) - Target must make a DC 11 Constitution save or take 3d6 poison damage
- Wyvern Poison (1,200 gp) - Target must make a DC 15 Constitution save or take 7d6 poison damage
- Spider Venom (200 gp) - Target must make a DC 11 Constitution save or take 2d8 poison damage
- Drow Poison (200 gp) - Target must make a DC 13 Constitution save or be poisoned for 1 hour

### Ingested Poisons (DC 18)
- Midnight Tears (1,500 gp) - No effect until midnight, then DC 17 Constitution save or take 9d6 poison damage
- Pale Tincture (250 gp) - Target must make a DC 16 Constitution save or take 1d6 poison damage and be poisoned for 24 hours
- Truth Serum (150 gp) - Target must make a DC 11 Constitution save or be unable to tell lies for 1 hour
- Assassin's Blood (150 gp) - Target must make a DC 10 Constitution save or take 1d12 poison damage and be poisoned for 24 hours
- Torpor (600 gp) - Target must make a DC 15 Constitution save or be poisoned and incapacitated for 4d6 hours

### Inhaled Poisons (DC 20)
- Burnt Othur Fumes (500 gp) - Target must make a DC 13 Constitution save or take 3d6 poison damage and be poisoned for 1 hour
- Malice (250 gp) - Target must make a DC 15 Constitution save or be poisoned and blinded for 1 hour
- Essence of Ether (300 gp) - Target must make a DC 13 Constitution save or be poisoned and unconscious for 8 hours
- Insanity Mist (2,500 gp) - Target must make a DC 15 Constitution save or be poisoned and confused for 1d10 hours
- Nightmare Vapor (900 gp) - Target must make a DC 16 Wisdom save or be frightened for 8 hours

### Contact Poisons (DC 25)
- Oil of Taggit (400 gp) - Target must make a DC 13 Constitution save or be poisoned and unconscious for 24 hours
- Purple Worm Poison (2,000 gp) - Target must make a DC 19 Constitution save or take 12d6 poison damage
- Dragon Bile (1,500 gp) - Target must make a DC 20 Constitution save or take 10d6 poison damage
- Black Lotus Extract (4,500 gp) - Target must make a DC 20 Constitution save or take 10d10 poison damage
- Basilisk Blood (1,800 gp) - Target must make a DC 18 Constitution save or begin to petrify

## Special Rules
- Failed checks may result in poisoning the crafter
- Working with toxic ingredients requires proper safety precautions
- Some poisons require specific environmental conditions
- Poisons have a shelf life depending on their ingredients and complexity

## Common Ingredients
- Venomous creature parts (fangs, stingers, glands)
- Toxic plants and fungi
- Mineral toxins
- Magical essences
- Preservative agents
