# Cooking Table

The Cooking Table is used for preparing beneficial meals and consumables. It requires cooking tools and fresh ingredients.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Cook's utensils
- **Proficiency Required**: Cook's utensils
- **Ability Check**: Wisdom (Survival)

## Crafting Process

1. Gather required ingredients
2. Prepare the ingredients properly
3. Cook according to the recipe
4. Make a Wisdom (Survival) check against the DC of the meal
5. Serve while fresh for maximum effect

## Crafting DCs

| Meal Type        | Crafting DC | Time Required | Effect Duration |
|------------------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Simple Meal      | 10          | 30 minutes    | 4 hours         |
| Hearty Meal      | 12          | 1 hour        | 8 hours         |
| Feast            | 15          | 3 hours       | 12 hours        |
| Special Cuisine  | 18          | 5 hours       | 24 hours        |
| Magical Delicacy | 20          | 8 hours       | 48 hours        |

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in inedible food
- Fresh ingredients provide better bonuses
- Some recipes require rare or exotic ingredients
- Effects stack with short rests but not long rests

## Common Ingredients

- Meats (various)
- Vegetables and fruits
- Herbs and spices
- Magical plants
- Special monster parts
