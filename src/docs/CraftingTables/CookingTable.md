The Cooking Table is used for preparing beneficial meals and treats. It requires cooking utensils and ingredients.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Cook's utensils
- **Proficiency Required**: Cook's utensils
- **Ability Check**: Wisdom (Survival)

## Crafting Process
1. Gather required ingredients
2. Prepare the workspace with proper equipment
3. Follow the recipe precisely
4. Make a Wisdom (Survival) check against the DC of the dish
5. Complete the cooking process

## Crafting DCs
| Dish Type | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Simple    | 10          | 1 hour        | 5         |
| Moderate  | 13          | 2 hours       | 15        |
| Complex   | 15          | 4 hours       | 30        |
| Exotic    | 18          | 8 hours       | 75        |
| Magical   | 20          | 12 hours      | 150       |

## Craftable Items

### Simple Dishes (DC 10)
- Hearty Stew (3 gp) - Provides nourishment for a full day
- Traveler's Bread (2 gp) - Stays fresh for 2 weeks
- Dried Meat (5 gp) - Lasts for months without spoiling
- Fortifying Broth (5 gp) - Grants advantage on Constitution saves against disease for 4 hours
- Energizing Tea (3 gp) - Reduces exhaustion effects for 2 hours

### Moderate Dishes (DC 13)
- Hunter's Feast (10 gp) - Grants +1 to Wisdom (Perception) checks for 8 hours
- Warrior's Meal (12 gp) - Grants temporary hit points equal to your level for 4 hours
- Fisherman's Delight (15 gp) - Grants water breathing for 10 minutes
- Mountaineer's Porridge (12 gp) - Resistance to cold damage for 1 hour
- Ranger's Pie (15 gp) - Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks for 8 hours

### Complex Dishes (DC 15)
- Heroic Roast (25 gp) - Grants +1 to Strength checks and saves for 4 hours
- Scholar's Soup (25 gp) - Grants +1 to Intelligence checks for 4 hours
- Diplomat's Delicacy (30 gp) - Grants +2 to Charisma (Persuasion) checks for 4 hours
- Assassin's Appetizer (30 gp) - Grants advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks for 1 hour
- Berserker's Brew (25 gp) - +1d4 damage on your next successful weapon attack

### Exotic Dishes (DC 18)
- Dragon Pepper Steak (70 gp) - Grants resistance to fire damage for 1 hour
- Fey-Touched Salad (65 gp) - Grants darkvision for 8 hours
- Underdark Mushroom Soup (60 gp) - Grants tremorsense 10 ft. for 1 hour
- Celestial Soufflé (75 gp) - Heals 2d8 hit points and removes one level of exhaustion
- Kraken Ink Pasta (70 gp) - Grants water breathing and swimming speed of 30 ft. for 1 hour

### Magical Dishes (DC 20)
- Phoenix Flame Cake (150 gp) - If you would drop to 0 hit points, instead drop to 1 hit point (once)
- Chimera Cheese Platter (140 gp) - Grants the effects of a Polymorph spell for 10 minutes (limited to CR 1 beasts)
- Sphinx's Riddle Rolls (145 gp) - Grants advantage on Intelligence checks for 8 hours
- Unicorn Milk Custard (150 gp) - Cures any disease and removes any poison
- Dragon's Delight (200 gp) - Grants immunity to one damage type for 10 minutes

## Special Rules
- Failed checks may result in inedible food
- Some dishes require special ingredients from specific regions
- Magical dishes may provide temporary benefits (advantage on certain checks, temporary hit points, etc.)
- Dishes have a shelf life depending on their ingredients and preparation method

## Common Ingredients
- Meats and fish
- Vegetables and fruits
- Herbs and spices
- Grains and flours
- Magical ingredients for special effects
