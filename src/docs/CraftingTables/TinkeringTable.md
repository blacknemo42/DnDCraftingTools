The Tinkering Table is used for creating small mechanical gadgets, clockwork devices, and intricate mechanisms. It requires specialized tools and precision.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Tinker's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Tinker's tools
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Investigation) or Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)

## Crafting Process
1. Design the gadget or device
2. Gather required small components
3. Assemble the components with precision
4. Make an Intelligence (Investigation) or Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check against the DC of the device
5. Test and calibrate the mechanism

## Special Rules
- Tinkering requires good lighting and a stable work surface
- Failed checks may result in non-functional or unpredictable devices
- Some devices require regular maintenance or winding
- Advanced and masterwork devices may require rare or magical components
- Gnomes and rock gnomes in particular have advantage on tinkering checks

## Common Materials
- Small metal components (gears, springs, levers)
- Fine tools for detailed work
- Clockwork mechanisms
- Small gemstones for precision components
- Special oils and lubricants
- Miniature power sources (springs, magical crystals)
