# Tinkering Table

The Tinkering Table is used for creating small gadgets and contraptions. It requires specialized tools, small components, and creative thinking.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Tinker's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Tinker's tools
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Investigation)

## Crafting Process

1. Design the gadget with detailed schematics
2. Gather required small components
3. Assemble the gadget with precision
4. Make an Intelligence (Investigation) check against the DC of the gadget
5. Test and refine the mechanism

## Crafting DCs

| Gadget Type     | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|-----------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Simple Gadget   | 10          | 1 day         | 25        |
| Useful Tool     | 12          | 2 days        | 50        |
| Complex Gadget  | 15          | 5 days        | 150       |
| Advanced Device | 18          | 10 days       | 500       |
| Masterwork      | 20          | 20 days       | 2,000     |

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in a malfunctioning gadget
- Different materials provide different properties
- Some gadgets require additional components from other crafting disciplines
- Magical gadgets require additional enchanting steps

## Common Materials

- Various small metals (copper, brass, etc.)
- Gears, springs, and other tiny components
- Specialized tools for precision work
- Lubricants and binding agents
- Small power sources (clockwork, magical, etc.)

*Reference: Crafting 1.0.6 - Free Version*
