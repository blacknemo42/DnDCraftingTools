# Leatherworking Table

The Leatherworking Table is used for creating leather armor and accessories. It requires specialized tools and quality hides.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Leatherworker's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Leatherworker's tools
- **Ability Check**: Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)

## Crafting Process

1. Prepare and tan the leather hides
2. Cut patterns according to design specifications
3. Stitch and assemble the pieces
4. Make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check against the DC of the item
5. Complete the finishing and treatment process

## Crafting DCs

| Item Type        | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|------------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Simple Accessory | 10          | 1 day         | 10        |
| Complex Accessory| 12          | 2 days        | 25        |
| Light Armor      | 15          | 5 days        | 45        |
| Medium Armor     | 18          | 10 days       | 150       |
| Special Item     | 20          | 15 days       | 500       |

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in wasted materials
- Different creature hides provide different properties
- Some items require additional components from other crafting disciplines
- Magical leather items require additional enchanting steps

## Common Materials

- Various animal hides (cow, deer, etc.)
- Exotic creature hides (dragon, displacer beast, etc.)
- Tanning agents and dyes
- Stitching materials
- Waterproofing and protective oils
