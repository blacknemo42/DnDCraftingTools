The Leatherwork Table is used for creating leather goods, including armor, bags, and various accessories. It requires specialized tools and quality leather.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Leatherworker's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Leatherworker's tools
- **Ability Check**: Dexterity (Leatherworker's tools)

## Crafting Process
1. Prepare the leather by cleaning, stretching, and treating
2. Cut the leather according to patterns
3. Stitch and assemble the pieces
4. Make a Dexterity (Leatherworker's tools) check against the DC of the item
5. Apply finishing touches and treatments

## Special Rules
- The quality of leather affects the final product
- Exotic leathers (dragon hide, displacer beast, etc.) can provide special properties
- Failed checks may result in wasted materials
- Some magical items require additional components or enchantments

## Common Materials
- Standard leather (cow, deer, goat)
- Exotic leather (dragon, displacer beast, owlbear)
- Tanning agents and oils
- Thread and needles
- Buckles, clasps, and other metal components
- Dyes and paints for decoration
