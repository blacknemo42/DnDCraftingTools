# Scroll Table

The Scroll Table is used for creating magical scrolls. It requires specialized writing tools, magical inks, and parchment.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Calligrapher's supplies
- **Proficiency Required**: Arcana skill
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Arcana)

## Crafting Process

1. Prepare the magical parchment
2. Create special inks from magical components
3. Transcribe the spell with precise arcane symbols
4. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check against the DC of the spell level
5. Complete the binding and sealing process

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in a scroll with unpredictable effects
- The caster must know the spell being transcribed
- Spell components are consumed during the creation process
- Scrolls can be used by anyone with the spell on their class list

## Common Materials

- High-quality parchment
- Magical inks (various colors)
- Rare components specific to the spell
- Binding materials
- Sealing wax with magical properties
