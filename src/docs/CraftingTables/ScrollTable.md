The Scroll Table is used for creating magical scrolls containing spells. It requires specialized tools, inks, and parchment.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Calligrapher's supplies
- **Proficiency Required**: Arcana
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Arcana)

## Crafting Process
1. Gather required materials (special inks, parchment, etc.)
2. Prepare the workspace with proper equipment
3. Transcribe the spell with precise arcane symbols
4. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check against the DC of the scroll
5. Complete the binding process to seal the magic

## Special Rules
- You must know the spell or have access to it in written form
- Failed checks may result in wasted materials
- Three consecutive failures results in loss of all materials
- Scrolls can be used by anyone who can cast spells, even if the spell is not on their spell list
- A scroll is consumed when used

## Common Materials
- High-quality parchment
- Magical inks (made from various components)
- Rare minerals for pigments
- Binding materials
- Spell components specific to the spell being transcribed
