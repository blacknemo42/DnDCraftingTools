The Woodcarving Table is used for creating wooden items, tools, and weapons. It requires specialized tools and quality wood.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Woodcarver's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Woodcarver's tools
- **Ability Check**: Dexterity (Woodcarver's tools)

## Crafting Process
1. Select and prepare appropriate wood
2. Rough cut the basic shape
3. Carve details and refine the shape
4. Make a Dexterity (Woodcarver's tools) check against the DC of the item
5. Sand, finish, and treat the wood

## Special Rules
- The type of wood affects the final product's properties
- Magical woods (from enchanted forests, etc.) can provide special properties
- Failed checks may result in wasted materials
- Some magical items require additional components or enchantments
- Seasoning wood properly takes time but improves quality

## Common Materials
- Standard woods (oak, pine, maple, ash)
- Exotic woods (ironwood, silverwood, darkwood)
- Oils and varnishes for finishing
- Carving and whittling tools
- Inlay materials (bone, metal, gems)
- Magical essences for enchanted items
