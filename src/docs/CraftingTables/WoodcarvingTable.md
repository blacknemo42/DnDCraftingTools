# Woodcarving Table

The Woodcarving Table is used for shaping wood into weapons and items. It requires specialized carving tools and quality wood.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Woodcarver's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Woodcarver's tools
- **Ability Check**: Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)

## Crafting Process

1. Select and prepare appropriate wood
2. Rough cut the basic shape
3. Carve details and refine the shape
4. Make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check against the DC of the item
5. Complete the sanding and finishing process

## Crafting DCs

| Item Type        | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|------------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Simple Item      | 10          | 1 day         | 5         |
| Simple Weapon    | 12          | 2 days        | 15        |
| Complex Item     | 15          | 4 days        | 30        |
| Shield           | 15          | 5 days        | 50        |
| Special Item     | 18          | 7 days        | 100       |

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in wasted materials
- Different woods provide different properties
- Some items require additional components from other crafting disciplines
- Magical wooden items require additional enchanting steps

## Common Materials

- Various woods (oak, maple, pine, etc.)
- Exotic woods (ironwood, silverwood, etc.)
- Carving tools of various sizes
- Sanding materials
- Finishing oils and varnishes
