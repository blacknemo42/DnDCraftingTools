The Alchemy Table is used for creating potions, oils, and other alchemical items. It requires specialized tools and ingredients.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Alchemist's supplies
- **Proficiency Required**: Alchemist's supplies
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine)

## Crafting Process
1. Gather required ingredients
2. Prepare the workspace with proper equipment
3. Follow the recipe precisely
4. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Medicine) check against the DC of the potion
5. Complete the brewing process

## Special Rules
- Failed checks may result in wasted ingredients
- Three consecutive failures results in loss of all materials
- Some potions require special ingredients from specific monsters or locations
- Potions have a shelf life depending on their ingredients and complexity

## Common Ingredients
- Herbs and plants
- Monster parts
- Mineral components
- Magical essences
- Distilled water or alcohol base
