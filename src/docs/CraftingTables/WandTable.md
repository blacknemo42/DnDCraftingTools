# Wand Crafting Table

The Wand Crafting Table is used for creating magical wands and staves. It requires specialized tools, magical wood, and arcane knowledge.

## Requirements

- **Tools Required**: Woodcarver's tools
- **Proficiency Required**: Arcana skill
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Arcana)

## Crafting Process

1. Select and prepare magical wood
2. Carve the wand with precise arcane patterns
3. Infuse the wand with magical energy
4. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check against the DC of the wand
5. Complete the binding and finishing process

## Crafting DCs

| Wand Type      | Crafting DC | Time Required | Cost (gp) |
|----------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Minor Wand     | 15          | 7 days        | 100       |
| Standard Wand  | 18          | 14 days       | 500       |
| Greater Wand   | 20          | 30 days       | 2,000     |
| Lesser Staff   | 22          | 60 days       | 5,000     |
| Standard Staff | 25          | 90 days       | 10,000    |
| Greater Staff  | 28          | 120 days      | 50,000    |

## Special Rules

- Failed checks may result in a wand with unpredictable effects
- The crafter must be able to cast at least one spell
- Different woods provide different magical properties
- Wands require attunement by a spellcaster

## Common Materials

- Magical woods (oak, yew, rowan, etc.)
- Magical creature parts (heartstrings, feathers, etc.)
- Precious gems for focusing elements
- Binding materials
- Magical oils for finishing
