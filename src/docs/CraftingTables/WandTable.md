The Wand Table is used for creating magical wands that can cast spells. It requires specialized tools, rare woods, and magical components.

## Requirements
- **Tools Required**: Woodcarver's tools, Arcana proficiency
- **Proficiency Required**: Woodcarver's tools, Arcana
- **Ability Check**: Intelligence (Arcana)

## Crafting Process
1. Gather required materials (special woods, magical components, etc.)
2. Prepare the workspace with proper equipment
3. Carve the wand with precise arcane symbols
4. Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check against the DC of the wand
5. Complete the binding process to infuse the magic

## Special Rules
- You must be a spellcaster to craft a wand
- Failed checks may result in wasted materials
- Three consecutive failures results in loss of all materials
- Some wands require attunement by a spellcaster
- Wands have a limited number of charges that replenish at dawn

## Common Materials
- Special woods (yew, oak, ash, rowan)
- Magical creature parts (phoenix feather, unicorn hair, dragon heartstring)
- Gems for focusing magical energy
- Rare metals for binding components
- Spell components specific to the wand's purpose
